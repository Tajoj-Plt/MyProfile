The FawtraSmart project

One system for
the whole business

FawtraSmart is an ERP system that brings a company’s daily operations into one place, with a web dashboard for the office and a mobile app for teams in the field. Here is how each part works.

01
01Web dashboard

FawtraSmart
Web Dashboard

The full system in the browser, for managers and office teams

Teams sign in with their company email to reach their dashboard. It brings together real-time business analytics, integrated inventory management, advanced financial reporting, multi-user collaboration, and enterprise-grade security, and it works in Arabic, English, Urdu, Hindi, and Bengali.

Real-time analyticsInventoryFinancial reportsMulti-userSecure sign-inFive languages

02Mobile app

FawtraSmart
Mobile App

The system in your pocket, for sales reps and teams on the move

The app opens with a branded splash screen and a simple sign-in. Users can switch between Arabic and English and between light and dark themes, and each person sees the modules their role gives them access to, with notifications for what needs their attention.

Arabic & EnglishDark modeRole-based accessNotificationsRight-to-left

03
03System modules

Five modules,
one system

Everything a growing business runs on, in one place

The home screen greets each user and lists the modules available to them today: sales management for sales and invoices, customer management for customers and loyalty, inventory management for stock and its movements, finance and accounts for journal entries and accounts, and HR management for employees and payroll. The modules are part of one integrated system, so every team works from the same data.
